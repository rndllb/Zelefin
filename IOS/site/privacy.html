Zelefin

Privacy Policy

Zelefin is a native Jellyfin and Silo client for iPhone, iPad, and Android. Emby and Plex support is planned and is not in the current app. It talks to the media server you type in. There is no Zelefin account and no Zelefin Plus.

What stays on your devices

Sign-in details, library cache, downloads, and settings live on your iPhone, iPad, or Android device. Playback goes to your Jellyfin or Silo server. When Emby or Plex sign-in ships, the same rule applies. We do not operate that server, and we do not receive your library files or media-server password.

Notifications

If you install the Zelefin plugin on Jellyfin and allow alerts, the app registers a push token with that plugin. iPhone and iPad use Apple Push Notification service. Android uses Firebase Cloud Messaging. The plugin sends alerts through Zelefin’s push service so they can arrive while the app is closed, including dashboard Display Messages. That service holds push credentials for the Zelefin apps. It receives device tokens and notification text from your plugin. It does not receive your Jellyfin password or library files. Silo has no Zelefin plugin; this device checks that server itself.

Optional connections

If you sign in to Seerr, Sonarr, Radarr, or Open Subtitles from Settings, those credentials stay on this device and are sent only to the address you enter.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use your library for advertising.
  • We do not require a cloud login to use Zelefin with your own server.
