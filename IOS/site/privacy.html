Zelefin

Privacy Policy

Zelefin is a native Jellyfin and Silo client for iPhone and iPad. It talks to the media server you type in. There is no Zelefin account.

What stays on your devices

Sign-in details, library cache, downloads, and settings live on your iPhone or iPad. Playback goes to your Jellyfin or Silo server. We do not operate that server, and we do not receive your library files or media-server password.

Notifications

If you install the Zelefin plugin on Jellyfin and allow alerts, the app registers an Apple Push token with that plugin. The plugin sends alerts through Zelefin’s push service so they can arrive while the app is closed. That service holds Apple Push credentials for the Zelefin app. It receives device tokens and notification text from your plugin. It does not receive your Jellyfin password or library files. Silo has no Zelefin plugin; this device checks that server itself.

Purchases

Zelefin Plus is sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your payment card number.

Optional connections

If you sign in to Seerr, Sonarr, Radarr, or Open Subtitles from Settings, those credentials stay on this device and are sent only to the address you enter.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use your library for advertising.
  • We do not require a cloud login to use Zelefin with your own server.
